// thesis
Humans cooperate within a limited radius — roughly Dunbar's number. Beyond that, institutions mediate trust. But institutions are failing: they're slow, opaque, and often captured by the interests they were built to check.

Smart contracts and tokenization can expand the cooperation radius. Not by replacing trust, but by encoding agreements so precisely that strangers can cooperate on complex, long-horizon endeavors — livestock investment across borders, fractional infrastructure ownership, community governance — with the same confidence they'd extend to a neighbor.

We build the tools that make this possible.
