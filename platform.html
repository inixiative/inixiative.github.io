the problem

Fiat quietly taxes everyone who saves. Whoever stands closest to the printer spends new money at old prices; wage earners and savers absorb the difference. That's not a bug of the system — it is the system.

Crypto solved half of it. Bitcoin is a genuine store of value — and precisely because it appreciates, nobody wants to spend it. Stablecoins move fast — and import every vagary of the fiat they're pegged to. One instrument keeps being asked to do two incompatible jobs.

And ownership lost its voice. When your savings sit in index funds, a custodian votes your shares. Three firms cast roughly a quarter of the votes across the S&P 500 — with other people's money and no accountability to it.

the design — two instruments, one job each

the transaction layer

A currency backed by energy: minted when a kilowatt-hour is produced, burned when it's consumed. Supply grows with real productive capacity — thermodynamics is the central bank, and thermodynamics doesn't do bailouts. This is the liquidity layer of the system.

the store of value

An index of every real asset on the platform. A small tax on every trade accrues into it — the same proven mechanic as an exchange liquidity pool, generalized to real-world assets. It appreciates because the marketplace is used, not because someone minted it.

how it works
01

tokenize

Bring a real asset or project onto the platform — its ownership becomes tradeable tokens with rules the asset's own community configures.

02

discover the price

At milestones, a slice of the asset goes to sealed-bid auction where honesty is the winning strategy. The platform co-buys at the price a real, motivated bidder just proved — it never asserts a valuation of its own.

03

mint against reality

New index tokens are created only when a real asset actually enters the basket, balanced on both sides of the ledger at that instant — the same creation mechanic ETFs use to move trillions without inflating anything.

04

exit honestly

When an asset sells back into the world, every owner is paid their share. The index's share buys back tokens on the open market — never above their backing — and burns them. Supply contracts in step with reality, in both directions.

And everything in between sits in escrow. Whenever value is recognized before it's realized — a milestone funded, future capacity pre-sold — it's held against fully-escrowed collateral and released only as delivery verifies. Nothing on the platform pays out on a promise.

governance

Your stake is your vote — no custodian in between. Voting power is the platform tokens you actually hold, cast directly — weight that can only be acquired at a price someone really paid, never from a self-reported valuation. Concentrated capital that is visible and accountable, instead of concentrated capital laundered through a proxy department.

Each project governs itself. Platform-wide votes set platform rules; a project's own token holders govern that project. Nobody outvotes a community on its own business.
