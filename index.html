// thesis
Humans cooperate within a limited radius — roughly Dunbar's number. Beyond that, institutions mediate trust. But institutions are failing: they're slow, opaque, and often captured by the interests they were built to check.

Software can expand the cooperation radius. By encoding agreements precisely enough that strangers can cooperate on complex, long-horizon endeavors — cross-border investment, fractional ownership, community governance — with the same confidence they'd extend to a neighbor.

We build the tools that make this possible.
