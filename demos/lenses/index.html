  1. 01 / DELEGATE

    Narrow the lens

    The platform defines the surface. Organizations narrow it for customers. Bound identities and ABAC, RBAC, and ReBAC policies determine access.

  2. 02 / REASON

    Connect two field maps

    Infer an ownership handoff from shipment notes. Bridge the temporary EAV table to source data, then combine facts, nested groups, and ranked windows in one predicate.

  3. 03 / VERIFY

    Push the boundaries

    Try a hidden field or another customer’s data. Revoke access and see stale results. Expire the facts and evaluate again. Definitions and predicates are serializable JSON.
