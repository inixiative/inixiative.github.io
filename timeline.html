the inixiative platform — timeline

How the system works, and where the value goes.

Phase by phase: what every party puts in, what every party gets out. If a flow isn't on this page, it doesn't exist — that's the design.

phase 0

Genesis — the basket is seeded

Early believers buy tokens directly from the platform at net asset value plus a disclosed issuance fee. Every dollar of NAV goes into the basket as cash. The token is fully backed from the first block — by design, it is boring.

early holders

cash, at NAV + disclosed fee tokens backed 1:1 by the basket

the basket

— 100% cash reserve, NAV $1.00

platform operator

builds & runs the rails the disclosed issuance fee only
phase 1

Building — inixiatives arrive

Founders tokenize real assets and projects. They raise from their own investors, in any currency, held in platform escrow and released as milestones verify. Their tokens trade on the marketplace; a small tax on each trade splits between the basket (holders' share) and the treasury (operations).

inixiative founders

service fees for the rails a cap table, escrowed funding, their own governance

their investors

capital, any currency asset tokens; funds release only as delivery verifies

traders

a small trade tax a liquid market in real-asset tokens

token holders

— basket accretes the tax share — NAV creeps up with usage
phase 2

First IPO — the basket becomes real

An inixiative hits its milestone and elects to IPO. A sealed-bid auction — where honest bidding is the winning strategy — proves the price. Bids settle in platform tokens; bidders who need them can buy fresh tokens from the platform at NAV. Only the slice delivered into the basket mints new tokens, balanced on both sides at the proven price.

founders

a slice of their asset liquidity, a market-proven price, platform tokens

auction winner

tokens, at the second-highest price a private stake in a real asset

the basket

cash or treasury tokens (co-buy, when funded) real-asset slices at an arm's-length price

token holders

— the token now holds real assets, not just cash
phase 3

The flywheel — usage compounds

More inixiatives, more trades, more IPOs. Tax flow funds the treasury's standing bid and its co-buys. Staggered auction checkpoints keep every mark honest — no single price is ever load-bearing. The token appreciates for exactly one reason: value entered the basket that no mint matched.

token holders

— appreciation = basket growth unmatched by mint

everyone with a stake

engagement direct governance — held tokens vote, no custodian in between

platform operator

runs auctions, escrow, checkpoints published service fees — nothing else
phase 4

Exit — supply tracks reality

An asset sells off-platform, in whatever currency the buyer pays. Every owner is paid their share. The basket's share buys back tokens on the open market — never above NAV — and burns them. Supply contracts in step with the value that left. Losses pass through the same pipe: the token tracks, in both directions, always.

founders & investors

their asset their share of the sale, in the sale currency

holders who want out

tokens, sold into the buyback cash — a self-selected payout

holders who stay

— a larger share of a smaller, honest basket
every fee, in the open

Every fee is a published, governed parameter. If the platform earns it, it's on this table.

fee charged on goes to
issuance fee buying tokens from the platform (disclosed % over NAV) platform maintenance
tokenization fee bringing an asset onto the rails platform maintenance
escrow fee holding & milestone-releasing raised funds platform maintenance
trade tax marketplace trades of asset tokens split: basket (holders) + treasury (operations)
auction fee running an IPO / checkpoint auction treasury

Portability is priced, not banned. Inixiatives that keep their tokens tradeable only on the platform pay discounted service fees — their trade flow funds the whole system. Inixiatives that choose freely portable tokens pay full fees up front, because their off-platform trades contribute nothing. Either way: the choice is the inixiative's, and the price of it is public.
